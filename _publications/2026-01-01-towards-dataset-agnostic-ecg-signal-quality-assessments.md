This work develops an adaptive ECG signal-quality assessment designed to transfer across psychophysiological datasets without extensive dataset-specific tuning or large manually annotated training sets. It evaluates signal quality under varied recording conditions and examines how artefacts can affect downstream heart-rate-variability features and emotion classification.
