## Abstract

Seizures are both the most common neurological emergency afflicting neonates and the most difficult to detect clinically. Currently, the monitoring of a multi-channel electroencephalogram (EEG) is the gold standard for seizure detection. The accurate analysis of this physiological data requires a neurophysiologist with expertise in neonatal EEG. The provisioning of this expertise on a continuous basis can be challenging for medical facilities. In this paper, we describe a cloud-based platform capable of supporting clinicians through the creation of expert knowledge repositories. While the platform is considered general purpose, in this work it is applied specifically to neonatal EEG.
