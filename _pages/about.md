I am a Lecturer in Computer Science at **Munster Technological University (MTU), Cork**, and Programme Coordinator of the M.Sc. in Software Architecture & Design. My research is on engineering AI-intensive software that people can trust, with most of the applied work in healthcare.

## Research themes

- **Explainable and fair AI:** model-agnostic explanation, fairness auditing and intersectional bias
- **Generative AI:** training and evaluating models that synthesise biomedical images
- **Human factors and affective computing:** modelling emotion, personality and stress from physiological and behavioural data

More on the [Research](/research/) page; papers are on [Publications](/publications/).

## Recent

- **2026–2028:** co-supervising the [XFAIR-MED](/research/#xfair-med) postdoctoral fellowship on fairness auditing for breast-cancer AI (MSCA COFUND ROSETTA)
- **2026:** Special Sessions Chair, World Conference on Explainable Artificial Intelligence (xAI 2026)
- **2026:** papers in *Journal of Psychophysiology*, *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control* and the *Journal of Open Source Software*
- **2026:** Eleonora Blazhko's final-year project won Best Poster at AI in Health 2026
