I have lectured in software development and AI at undergraduate and postgraduate level since 2012, and at **Munster Technological University** since 2015. I have coordinated the **M.Sc. in Software Architecture & Design** since 2019.

## Teaching

**Currently teaching**

- **Knowledge Representation & Reasoning** (Level 9; module author)
- **Interactive Data Visualisation** (Level 8; module author)

**Previously designed or delivered**

- Analytic & Scientific Programming (Level 9; module author)
- Data Management Systems (Level 9)
- Advanced Web Publishing (Level 8; module author)
- Server-side Web Frameworks (Level 8)
- Object-Oriented Programming (Level 8)
- Server-side Web Development (Level 7; module author)
- Emerging Technologies (Level 7)

My teaching is research-led and practical. Students work with Python, Jupyter, Git/GitHub and cloud development environments, and are expected to explain why a method behaves as it does and where it stops working. I am currently revising assessment for a setting in which generative AI is freely available to students.

## Research supervision

I supervise research at PhD, M.Sc. and final-year undergraduate level, across explainable AI, generative AI, affective computing and software architecture. Much of this work has gone on to be published.

### PhD students

- **Zachary Dair** (current): complex adaptive systems and psychophysiological data, including dataset-agnostic ECG signal-quality assessment.
- **Muhammad Muneeb Saad** (2025): training challenges in generative AI, mode collapse and biomedical image augmentation. Published in *Artificial Intelligence Review* and *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control*; recognised in the Lero Director's Prizes.
- **Urja Pawar** (2024): model-agnostic explainable AI and neighbourhood-based evaluation of explanations. Published in *IEEE Open Journal of the Computer Society* and PMLR.
- **Ryan Donovan** (2023): affective computing and the relationship between personality traits and basic emotions. Published in the *Journal of Personality*.
