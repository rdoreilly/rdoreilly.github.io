## Teaching approach

My teaching is research-led and practice-focused. I combine conceptual foundations with implementation, experimentation and critical evaluation. Students work with reproducible computational workflows using **Python, Jupyter, Git/GitHub and cloud-based development environments**, and are expected to connect technical results to the assumptions and limitations of the methods they use.

In AI modules, I place particular emphasis on modelling problems clearly, understanding algorithmic trade-offs, evaluating evidence, and explaining why a method behaves as it does rather than treating software libraries as black boxes. Current curriculum development also considers how assessment should evolve in an environment where generative AI is readily available.
